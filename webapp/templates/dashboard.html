Your Private Trading Infrastructure

This dashboard gives you access to all 9 quantitative trading components. Each tool below explains what it does and lets you run it with your own parameters. Click "Run" to see results.

1. GARCH Volatility Model

Forecast market volatility

What it does: GARCH (Generalized Autoregressive Conditional Heteroskedasticity) predicts how volatile the market will be. High volatility = more risk and opportunity.

2. Smart Order Router

Find the best venue for your orders

What it does: When you want to buy/sell stocks, this finds the best exchange (NYSE, NASDAQ, Dark Pools) to get the best price and fastest execution.

3. Execution Algorithms

TWAP, VWAP, Implementation Shortfall

What it does: Breaks large orders into smaller pieces to minimize market impact. TWAP spreads evenly over time, VWAP follows volume patterns, IS minimizes slippage.

4. Market Impact Model

Almgren-Chriss cost estimation

What it does: Estimates how much your trade will move the market price. Larger orders have more impact. Helps you decide how aggressively to trade.

5. Portfolio Optimization

Mean-Variance & Risk Parity

What it does: Finds the optimal mix of stocks to maximize returns for your risk tolerance. Mean-Variance maximizes Sharpe ratio, Risk Parity balances risk equally.

6. Monitoring Dashboard

Real-time P&L and positions

What it does: Tracks your profit/loss in real-time, monitors all positions, calculates drawdown, and shows exposure. Essential for live trading.

7. Alert System

Risk monitoring and notifications

What it does: Monitors your account and triggers alerts when thresholds are breached (e.g., P&L drops too much, drawdown exceeds limit). Protects you from big losses.

8. Sentiment Analysis

Analyze news and social media

What it does: Reads news headlines and determines if they're positive, negative, or neutral for stocks. Helps you understand market sentiment before trading.

9. Trading Engine

Core trading system

What it does: The heart of the system. Coordinates strategies, executes orders, manages risk. Can run in paper trading mode (simulated) or live mode.
